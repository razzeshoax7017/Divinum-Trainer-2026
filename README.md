## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Divinum**. This trainer enhances Divinum's fast-paced combat and exploration by providing optional control over player survivability, attack power, enemy behavior, and combo-focused gameplay. It is designed for single-player use and complements the game's weapons, special skills, dodges, blocks, parries, aerial attacks, bosses, and Rune-based builds.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Divinum's island, discover secrets, experiment with weapons and Runes, practice combat techniques, or reduce the difficulty of challenging encounters.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore Divinum's interconnected island areas, including forests, caves, catacombs, and sacred ruins. Use the trainer's defensive options when navigating hazardous sections or practicing movement around enemy groups.

### Tools & Inventory

Runes provide meaningful bonuses and combat modifiers that can change how Fjør approaches battles. Equip and experiment with different Rune combinations normally, then use the trainer to make testing builds safer and faster.

### World & Environment

Divinum rewards exploration with hidden secrets, collectible Runes, ancient locations, dangerous trials, and boss encounters. Use the trainer to explore at your own pace while preserving the game's platforming and discovery-focused structure.

### Combat & Weapons

Fjør fights with two distinct primary weapons and special skills. Combat includes launch attacks, aerial follow-ups, combos, dodges, blocks, and parries. Activate health, damage, enemy-control, or combo options according to whether you want relaxed exploration, build testing, or combat practice.
